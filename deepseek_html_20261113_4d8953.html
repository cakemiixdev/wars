HOW TO PLAY WARS

Break the enemy bed to win. Protect yours, then push across the bridge!

MOVE & EXPLORE

Use WASD or arrow keys. On touch screens, steer with the left joystick and look around with the right one. Cross the bridge to reach the middle and enemy island.

FIGHT & BUILD

Press Space or SWING to attack. Press F or PLACE to build with wood. Near the center chest, press F or SWING to open it.

SHOP & SURVIVE

Press E or SHOP to buy a sword, axe, or blocks. Watch your health: a knockout means a short respawn, then a brief spawn shield.

CHOOSE YOUR GAME

Pick 2D Stickmen or 3D Island Arena in the lobby or settings. In 3D, choose Sky Garden, Ember Rift, or Frostwood Peaks. Run across glowing rings for a speed boost. Choose Web for bots, or host a room and share its code.

TIP: Your base forge earns 8 tokens each second. Loot the middle chest for 80 tokens and an emerald; more emeralds spawn in the center. Spend emeralds on a stronger blade or bed plating.
