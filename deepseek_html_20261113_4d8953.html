HOW TO PLAY WARS

Break the enemy bed to win. Protect yours, then push across the bridge!

MOVE & EXPLORE

Use WASD or arrow keys. On touch screens, steer with the left joystick. Cross the bridge to reach the middle and enemy island.

FIGHT & BUILD

Press Space or SWING to attack. Press F or PLACE to build with wood. Near the center chest, press F or SWING to open it.

SHOP & SURVIVE

Press E or SHOP to buy a sword, axe, or blocks. Watch your health: a knockout means a short respawn, then a brief spawn shield.

CHOOSE YOUR GAME

Pick 2D Stickmen or 3D Island Arena in the lobby or settings. Choose Web for bots, or host a room and share its code.

TIP: You start with 500 tokens. The middle chest gives 100 more, and tokens are earned during a match.
